Penumbra
Vest

A fitted sleeveless vest with a structural shoulder ridge

Yarn weight
Worsted
Needle size
5.0 mm + 4.5 mm
Gauge
18 sts × 25 rows / 10 cm
Sizes
XS — 3XL (7 sizes)
Construction
Bottom-up, seamless
Ease
Zero ease
Difficulty
Intermediate

Penumbra is worked from the bottom up in the round to the underarm, then shaped flat through the armhole and neck. The back shoulders extend past the neck shaping, travelling over the top of the shoulder and down the front face — joined with a three-needle bind-off that leaves a clean, deliberate ridge visible on the front. Wide 1×1 twisted ribbing at the hem draws in at the hip. Knitted at zero ease, it sits close to the body and layers beautifully over a shirt or under a coat.

  • Three-needle bind-off (RS ridge)

How it's
constructed

Bottom-up, seamless — no sewing required

01
Hem & body

Cast on and work 17 cm of twisted 1×1 rib for the hem. Switch to stockinette and work in the round to the underarm. Both front and back are identical at this stage.

02
Divide & shape

Bind off underarm stitches to separate front and back. Each piece is now worked flat. Armhole shaping gives the opening its final form with bind-offs and decrease rows.

03
Neck & shoulders

Back neck is shaped first. The back shoulders extend over the top and down the front face. Front neck is shaped separately. The shoulder ridge is created with a three-needle bind-off on the RS.

04
Finishing

Pick up the neckband and armhole bands in twisted rib. Finish each with an Italian bind-off for a seamless, stretchy edge. Wet block to finished measurements.

Sizing guide

Penumbra is worked at zero ease — the finished bust measurement matches your actual chest measurement. All sizes are cropped, with total lengths from 40–54 cm. If you prefer a slightly more relaxed fit, go up one size.

Yarn

Yarn
recommendations

Recommended
KFO Heavy Merino

Knitting for Olive Heavy Merino — 100% merino, 200 m / 100 g. Exceptional stitch definition, beautiful drape, and an outstanding natural colour range. This is what the sample is knitted in.

Budget pick
Worsted merino blend

Any 100% merino or merino-blend worsted at approximately 200 m / 100 g. Look for good stitch definition — avoid very fluffy or loosely plied yarns which soften the twisted rib texture.

Luxury pick
Cashmere-merino worsted

A cashmere-merino blend in worsted weight gives exceptional softness and a beautiful subtle sheen. Requires gentle hand washing and careful blocking. Worth it.
